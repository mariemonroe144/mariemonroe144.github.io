You already know you need this.

Most people spend their lives surrounded by technology, noise and obligation, quietly forgetting what it feels like to be genuinely present. To use their hands. To sit beside a real fire. To hear their own voice alongside other voices, in woodland, without performance or pretence.

Rewilding is one day that moves through three distinct experiences. It is practical and it is profound. It is earthy and it is transformative. It will ask something of you, and it will give you something significant back.

You will not leave the same person who walked in.

Morning · With Andy Hall

Learn the skills of the wild.

Andy Hall has spent years deep in woodland learning the ancestral skills that kept human beings alive long before the modern world convinced us we no longer needed them. He teaches with the calm, generous authority of someone who genuinely loves this landscape and wants other people to feel at home in it.

This morning is one of the best things you will do with your hands all year. You will make fire from real materials. You will understand shelter. You will learn to read the woodland around you as a living language. And something will shift in the way you relate to the natural world. Something that does not go away when you get back in your car.

No experience needed. No fitness level required. Just show up.

  • Fire making
  • Woodland awareness and navigation
  • Shelter building with natural materials
  • Plant identification and foraging
  • Working with tools in the field

Then.

We slow down.

Lunch is provided. The morning falls away. The afternoon hasn't started yet. This is the space between. Eat. Talk. Rest. Let the woodland do what it does.

Bring an instrument if you play one.

Afternoon · With Marie Monroe

Gather around
the fire.

This is where things get interesting. And this is the part most people say they weren't prepared for.

Around the campfire, Marie Monroe leads an experience that people consistently describe as the most unexpectedly powerful thing they have ever done. Not because it is dramatic. Because it is real.

It begins with guided meditation. Not the version where you sit in a studio trying to switch your brain off. The version that happens outdoors, with the sound of the woodland and the warmth of real fire. The version that actually works.

There is guided movement. Following rhythm and feeling rather than instruction or choreography. The natural response of the body when you stop overriding it and let the afternoon take the lead.

There is connection around the fire. The letting go of things. If people bring instruments, music happens. The afternoon takes the shape of whoever is in it, and that is part of what makes it different every time.

The things you carried into the woodland tend to loosen around a fire in good company. By the time the afternoon ends, something has shifted. People describe it differently but the feeling is consistent. Clear. Lighter. More themselves. Connected to people who arrived as complete strangers just hours earlier.

  • Guided meditation outdoors
  • Earth connection
  • A guided movement experience
  • Music and sound
  • Campfire sitting and reflection
  • Letting go

The Teaching

Become more conscious of the reality you are creating.

This is the part that most people say stays with them longest. Not in a vague way. In the specific, practical way of someone who suddenly understands why they keep getting in their own way.

Marie's teaching on conscious reality creation begins with a question most people have never properly sat with: how much of what you experience as reality is actually a story you are running, unconsciously and automatically, based on beliefs you absorbed before you were old enough to choose them?

The answer, it turns out, is most of it.

The teaching works through the relationship between your thoughts, your beliefs, your sense of identity, your perception of what is possible, and the actions you take. And the way those invisible patterns create, reinforce and limit the life you are living.

This is not pseudoscience. It is not manifestation. It is a genuinely intelligent, grounded framework for understanding why your life looks the way it does, and what becomes available when you start making those patterns conscious rather than leaving them running on their own.

The Monday morning after this day will feel different.
